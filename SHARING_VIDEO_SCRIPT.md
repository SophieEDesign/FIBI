# How to Save to FiBi - Video Script

## Video Overview
A simple, clear tutorial showing how to save places from TikTok or Instagram into FiBi. Target length: 30–60 seconds.

**Platform note:** Film separate cuts for iPhone (paste) and Android (share sheet). iOS web apps do not appear in the system share sheet.

---

## Script — iPhone (Paste)

### Opening (0-5 seconds)
**Visual:** Show phone home screen with FiBi app icon  
**Narration (optional):** "Save places from TikTok and Instagram into FiBi"

### Step 1: Find Content (5-10 seconds)
**Visual:** Open TikTok/Instagram, find a place post  
**Narration (optional):** "Find a post you want to save"

### Step 2: Copy the link (10-20 seconds)
**Visual:** Tap Share → Copy link  
**Narration (optional):** "Copy the link"

### Step 3: Open Fibi and paste (20-35 seconds)
**Visual:** Open Fibi → Save a place → paste → preview loads  
**Narration (optional):** "Open Fibi, paste, and save"

### Closing (35-40 seconds)
**Visual:** Place appears in the grid  
**Narration (optional):** "That's it — your place is saved"

---

## Script — Android (Share sheet)

### Opening (0-5 seconds)
**Visual:** FiBi on the home screen (installed PWA)  
**Narration (optional):** "Save places from TikTok and Instagram into FiBi"

### Step 1: Find Content (5-10 seconds)
**Visual:** Open TikTok/Instagram, find a place post

### Step 2: Tap Share (10-15 seconds)
**Visual:** Tap the share button; share sheet opens

### Step 3: Select FiBi (15-25 seconds)
**Visual:** Choose FiBi in the share sheet  
**Narration (optional):** "Tap Share, then FiBi"

### Step 4: Review and save (25-35 seconds)
**Visual:** Add screen with URL pre-filled and preview  
**Narration (optional):** "Check the preview and save"

### Closing (35-40 seconds)
**Visual:** Place in the grid

---

## On-screen text (optional)
- iPhone: "Copy link → Open Fibi → Paste"
- Android: "Share → FiBi"
